Kazuki Ogitsuka (扇塚 和希)

researchmap / ORCID / dblp

I am a fourth-year Ph.D student in the five-year doctoral program in Informatics at The Graduate University for Advanced Studies, SOKENDAI (National Institute of Informatics). I am advised by Dr. Shuichi Hirahara.

I am interested in theoretical computer science, especially computational complexity. I am currently focusing on the computational complexity of lattice problems and their applications to cryptography.
